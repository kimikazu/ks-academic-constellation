A working map of where North American educational development units publish: short paper series written by center staff, teaching guides, and the journals of their professional networks. Each table lists the outlet first, then representative pieces.

北米の教育開発ユニット（EDU / CTL）がどこで発信しているかを整理する作業用マップです。センター職員が執筆する短い論考シリーズ、ティーチング・ガイド、専門職ネットワークの機関誌を扱います。各表では、まず媒体を、次に代表的な記事を挙げます。

## 1. Paper series issued by CTLs / センター発行の論考シリーズ

| Outlet / 媒体 | Issuer / 発行 | Notes / メモ |
|---|---|---|
| [CRLT Occasional Papers](https://crlt.umich.edu/resources/occasional) | University of Michigan, CRLT | The archetype of the "occasional paper" genre: short research summaries by CRLT staff and U-M faculty, published on a variable schedule. / 「Occasional Paper」の典型。CRLT 職員と教員による研究の要約。不定期刊行。 |
| Speaking of Teaching | Stanford University, CTL | Historical quarterly newsletter (1990s–2000s); no central archive page found yet. / 1990〜2000年代の季刊ニューズレター。まとまったアーカイブは未確認。 |

## 2. Teaching guides and tip sheets / ティーチング・ガイド、ティップシート

| Outlet / 媒体 | Issuer / 発行 | Notes / メモ |
|---|---|---|
| [Teaching Guides](https://www.vanderbilt.edu/tav/) | Vanderbilt University, Teaching@Vanderbilt (formerly the Center for Teaching, now part of AdvancED) | Topic guides summarizing best practices: pedagogies, assessment, classroom challenges, and contexts. / ベストプラクティスを要約したテーマ別ガイド。旧 Center for Teaching の作成。 |
| [Instructional Strategies](https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/designteach/teach/instructionalstrategies/index.html) | Carnegie Mellon University, Eberly Center | Strategies for lectures, discussions, case studies, labs, group projects, and more. / 講義・討論・ケース・実験・グループ課題などの方略。 |
| [Teaching Tips (tip sheets)](https://uwaterloo.ca/centre-for-teaching-excellence/) | University of Waterloo, Centre for Teaching Excellence (Canada) | 100+ short, practical tip sheets, e.g. [Lecturing Effectively](https://uwaterloo.ca/centre-for-teaching-excellence/catalogs/tip-sheets/lecturing-effectively). / 100 以上の短い実践的ティップシート。 |

## 3. Journals and series of professional networks / 専門職ネットワークの機関誌・シリーズ

| Outlet / 媒体 | Issuer / 発行 | Notes / メモ |
|---|---|---|
| [To Improve the Academy (TIA)](https://podnetwork.org/publications/to-improve-the-academy/) | POD Network (US) | Flagship peer-reviewed journal of educational development; diamond open access since 2020, published by Michigan Publishing. Back issues: [UNL Digital Commons](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/podimproveacad/). / 教育開発分野の旗艦誌。2020 年から無料の完全オープンアクセス。 |
| [Essays on Teaching Excellence](https://podnetwork.org/publications-backup/essays/) | POD Network (US) | Short essays published annually 1989–2011; free to share with attribution. / 1989〜2011 年に毎年刊行された短いエッセイ。 |
| [IDEA Papers](https://www.ideaedu.org/research-resources/idea-papers-series/) | IDEA (US) | Peer-reviewed series since 1979 on teaching, faculty evaluation, and course design; now archival. Archive also at [The IDEA Fund](https://idea-fund.org/idea-papers). / 1979 年からの査読付きシリーズ。現在は新規刊行なし。 |
| [The Canadian Journal for the Scholarship of Teaching and Learning (CJSoTL)](https://ojs.lib.uwo.ca/index.php/cjsotl_rcacea) | STLHE (Canada) | Bilingual (EN/FR) open-access SoTL journal. / 英仏二言語のオープンアクセス SoTL 誌。 |
| [Green Guides](https://www.stlhe.ca/resources/green-guides/) | STLHE (Canada) | Short handbooks on teaching and learning, modeled on the HERDSA guides. / HERDSA のガイドに倣った短いハンドブック。 |
| [Educational Development Guides](https://www.stlhe.ca/resources/educational-development-resources/educational-development-guides/) | Educational Developers Caucus (EDC), STLHE (Canada) | Guides for developers themselves, e.g. No. 1 *The Educational Developer's Portfolio* (2016), No. 3 *Centre Reviews* (2018). / 教育開発者自身のためのガイド。 |
